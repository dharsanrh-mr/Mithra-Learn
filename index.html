📝 Grammar

1. “Nenu” = I

Use Nenu to talk about yourself.

2. “Naaku” = To me / I need

Example: Naaku water kaavali = I need water.

3. “Repu” = Tomorrow

Example: Nenu repu vastanu = I will come tomorrow.
